Gameplay

A puzzle game where your play a lost trick or treater trying to collect candy. Explore a Halloween themed cube world and find your way back home.

Development

This was a prototype based on Nintendo's Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker made in Unity (C#). Finished after 3 months of development, with 8 to 10 hours spent working each week in between other classes.

What I did

  • As a programmer on the team, I worked on: player, camera, pickup, menu, bomb, UI and UX functionality.
  • Other noteworthy things would be, teaching myself how to create an icon font for storing very compact input prompt and UI symbols.
  • Also learning how to make my own input manager that handles dynamic input prompt UI switching when different types of controllers are used.
