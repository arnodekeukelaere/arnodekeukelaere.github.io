Gameplay

A Hack n' Slash game where you play a corrupt Viking raiding and pillaging a village using superhuman attacks. Made in Unity.

What I did

  • As a programmer on the team I generally focused on UX which consisted of designing and programming the functionality of: the menus, scoreboard, pickups, player combat and UI.
  • I also put together and polished most of the prototypes that had to be presented on a weekly basis to our teachers, making sure everyone's code functioned as a whole.
