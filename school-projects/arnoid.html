What I did

Development

  • A very simple remake of Arkanoid for Android, made whilst learning Cocos2d-x.
  • You can angle your shots using the gyroscope functionality of your device.
  • Side note: I mostly put my time into learning the API for Cocos2d-x during this project, so everything is horribly crammed in a single main function.
