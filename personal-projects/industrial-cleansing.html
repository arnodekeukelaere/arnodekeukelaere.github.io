Industrial Cleansing

Physics sim object cleaning game. You get 33 seconds to clean each batch of objects.

Release Year
2025
Platforms
PC
Technologies
C#, s&box

Info

A top-down 2.5D physics-based game where you use your mouse and keyboard to grab objects, move them to a central sink, and clean away strange “growths” using a variety of cleansing tools. Built in a weekend and refined over the following weeks and available free to play on s&box!

Tech

  • C#
  • s&box
  • Affinity Photo 2
  • Audacity
  • Various AI tools for productivity
