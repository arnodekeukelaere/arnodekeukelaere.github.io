Gameplay

One more reincarnation. The cycle of life and death starts anew. You have died, and a game of chance is all that stands between you and your soon-to-be vessel.

Development

Before this project, I took pride in making sure to only use AI tools for asking coding questions and minor assistance, but given how much AI-assisted coding tools are starting to creep into my daily workflow in my professional life, I decided to see how difficult it would be to make a game with a fully AI-generated codebase. The way I went about this was a bit ham-fisted, as I just prompted ChatGPT and/or Gemini to generate classes for me and hooked the output up myself in the s&box engine/editor, gradually bug fixing and expanding features.

By the end of the project, I realised that the problem-solving inherent to coding is my favourite part of game development. I guess you could say the journey is the fun part, so this project has left me wary of making fully AI-generated codebases for my personal projects in the future.
