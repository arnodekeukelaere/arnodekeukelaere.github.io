Gameplay

Players control a small blob-like alien that crash-lands on a mysterious planet. The objective is to:

  • Rescue friendly characters (friends)
  • Hop on enemies mercilessly
  • Escape the planet

Development Process

This was one of my early projects created with Stencyl game engine. The game was developed as a challenge to create a complete game independently using freeware tools, with only music being outsourced.

Art Style: Simple pixel art aesthetic

Inspirations: Kirby's Adventure, Super Meat Boy, They Bleed Pixels

Tools Used:

  • Stencyl (game development)
  • Adobe Photoshop (art creation)
  • Tiled (level design)
  • Bfxr (sound effects)
  • Audacity (audio editing)

Recognition

The game was submitted to the 2014 Newgrounds Stencyl Jam and served as motivation for pursuing formal education in game development.

Technical Details

  • Engine: Stencyl
  • Art Tools: Photoshop, Tiled
  • Audio Tools: Bfxr, Audacity
